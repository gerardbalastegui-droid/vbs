3. The key

To scout matches, VBS needs a key, which the author gives out. Without a key,
VBS only opens matches to view them.

- The key is personal and works on one computer only: the first one it is
  activated on. To change computers, ask the author.
- The key can be valid with no time limit or until a date, depending on how
  the author gives it to you. After that date it stops working, offline too.
- The author can deactivate or delete a key at any time. Once deactivated,
  deleted or expired, VBS goes back to only opening and viewing matches:
  your matches and the rest of your data are not touched.
- To activate the key, and then whenever there is a connection (when VBS opens
  and every six hours), VBS connects to the author's key server and sends only
  this: the key, an identifier of the computer (a digest computed from the
  Windows identifier, which is never sent) and the version of VBS. Nothing
  from your matches, your teams or any other data of yours.
- On the server, the key carries the name the author gave it, a note of
  theirs and, if it has one, the date until which it is valid, along with
  that identifier, the version and the dates of activation and of the last
  check. The IP address is not stored, although, as with any internet
  connection, the provider hosting the server (Cloudflare) sees it.
- Without a connection, the key stays valid for up to 14 days after the
  last check.


4. Your data is yours

The matches, teams, reports, videos and any other data you create with VBS
are yours to do with as you wish. They are stored on your computer: the
author receives none of them. The only things VBS sends to the author are
what section 3 describes, for the key, and its version when it checks for a
new one:

- The installed VBS checks for a new version on opening and every six
  hours, if there is a connection: it asks the author's server for a single
  file (which version is the latest) and tells it only the version of VBS.
  Nothing about the key, the computer or your data, and the server stores
  none of it; as in section 3, Cloudflare sees the IP address.
- A new version is only installed if you ask for it, and only if it carries
  the author's signature and is exactly the one the author published:
  otherwise VBS installs nothing. Before installing it, VBS backs up your
  settings, teams and key, and your matches and the rest of your data are
  not touched.
